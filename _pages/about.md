I am a Vector Ecologist trained in Medical Geography and Global Health
Epidemiology. I investigate how environmental conditions shape vector
ecologies, and how that can be related to public health and spatial
patterns of risk. My research integrates geography, community ecology,
and public health to understand how complex factors like climate change
influence mosquito and tick ecology.
